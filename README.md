🎯 Project Overview
This repository contains the final project analyzing operational efficiency and growth potential for Bean There Done That, a coffee roasting company. Through data-driven storytelling, we uncovered how the company can scale sustainably without additional capital investment—simply by improving coordination across departments.

Case Study: Are We Ready to Meet the Demand?
The project demonstrates how operational stability can mask underlying inefficiencies that prevent scalable growth. Our analysis revealed that Bean There Done That has 40% idle roasting capacity and significant coordination gaps between production, procurement, and sales teams.

📈 Key Performance Indicators Analyzed
Our analysis focused on five critical KPIs to assess operational balance:

Demand-Production Gap - Comparing customer orders to actual output

Overstock-to-Waste Rate - Percentage of inventory becoming scrap due to planning misalignment

Roaster Utilization - Actual capacity usage vs. total available capacity

Inventory Turnover - Speed at which produced goods are sold and replaced

Supplier Reliability - Consistency of supplier deliveries against their averages

🔍 Major Findings
56.3% roaster utilization with 617 kg/month missed capacity (74% growth potential)

Demand peaked between August-December, but production remained flat

Inventory turnover dropped from 10.9x (February) to 3.9x (May), locking capital in stock

Scrap volume reached 21,000 kg during peak stock periods

Supplier variability drives inventory fluctuations, with reliability scores ranging from 17-57/100

💡 Key Insights
Root Causes Identified
Production, sourcing, and sales operate in silos with no shared forecasting

Procurement is reactive rather than forecast-based

Single-shift roasting limits flexibility despite available capacity

Growth is constrained by coordination, not machine capacity

Strategic Recommendations
Timeframe	Focus	Action
Short-term	KPI alignment & team visibility	Stop operational waste
Mid-term	Supplier reliability & inventory flow	Build stable growth foundation
Long-term	Capacity expansion (if demand sustains +30%)	Enable scalable, sustainable growth
