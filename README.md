## 🎯 About The Project

This repository showcases our final project for the **Storytelling with Data** course at HAN University of Applied Sciences, which earned a **grade of 8.3**. 

We analyzed **Bean There Done That**, a coffee roasting company, to uncover how operational stability can mask underlying inefficiencies that prevent scalable growth. Our data-driven approach revealed that the company could achieve **74% growth potential** without additional capital investment—simply through improved coordination.

### The Central Question

> **Are We Ready to Meet the Demand?**

Through comprehensive data analysis and visualization, we discovered that Bean There Done That has significant untapped capacity and coordination gaps between production, procurement, and sales teams.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators Analyzed

We developed five critical KPIs to assess operational balance and growth readiness:

| KPI | Description | Purpose |
|-----|-------------|---------|
| 🎯 **Demand-Production Gap** | Customer orders vs. actual output | Identifies unmet market demand |
| ♻️ **Overstock-to-Waste Rate** | % of inventory becoming scrap | Reveals planning misalignment |
| ⚙️ **Roaster Utilization** | Actual vs. total capacity usage | Shows growth potential |
| 📦 **Inventory Turnover** | Speed of stock replacement | Measures capital efficiency |
| 🚚 **Supplier Reliability** | Delivery consistency score | Assesses supply chain stability |

---

## 🔍 Major Findings

### Production & Capacity

- **56.3% average roaster utilization** → 40% idle capacity available
- **617 kg/month** missed capacity equals **74% untapped growth potential**
- Production remains flat despite fluctuating demand
- Single-shift operation limits flexibility

### Demand & Sales

- Demand peaked sharply **August-December** (110,000 kg)
- Production stayed flat at ~80,000 kg/month
- Growing backlog and unmet orders during peak season
- High-volume, low-margin products dominate sales mix

### Inventory Management

- Inventory turnover **dropped from 10.9x** (Feb) **to 3.9x** (May)
- Peak stock reached **~65,000 kg** (May-August)
- Capital locked in slow-moving inventory
- **21,000 kg scrap volume** during peak periods (5-7% yield loss)

## 💡 Key Insights

### Root Causes Identified

**Critical Issues:**

- ❌ **Siloed Operations**: Production, sourcing, and sales lack coordination
- ❌ **Reactive Procurement**: Purchasing not based on demand forecasts
- ❌ **Limited Flexibility**: Single-shift roasting constrains responsiveness
- ✅ **Hidden Opportunity**: Growth constrained by coordination, NOT capacity

### The Bottom Line

> **Growth potential lies in better coordination, not new machines.**

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

### Implementation Roadmap

#### 🏃 Short-term (0-3 months)
**Focus:** KPI Alignment & Team Visibility

**Actions:**
- Implement shared dashboards
- Weekly cross-functional meetings
- Unified demand forecasting

**Expected Impact:**
- ✅ Stop operational waste
- ✅ Increase team visibility
- ✅ Reduce overstock by 30%

#### 🚶 Mid-term (3-12 months)
**Focus:** Supply Chain Optimization

**Actions:**
- Forecast-based procurement
- Supplier reliability program
- Inventory flow optimization

**Expected Impact:**
- ✅ Stable growth foundation
- ✅ Improve turnover to 7-8x
- ✅ Reduce scrap by 50%

#### 🎯 Long-term (12+ months)
**Focus:** Capacity Expansion

**Actions:**
- Scale only if demand sustains +30%
- Multi-shift operations
- Automated scheduling systems

**Expected Impact:**
- ✅ Scalable growth model
- ✅ 70%+ utilization rate
- ✅ Sustainable profitability
